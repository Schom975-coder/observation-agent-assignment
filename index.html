Observation agent

A system that runs inside Claude Code, reads all 3,240 messages of an email archive from Enron, finds the work that recurs, prices what could be automated at the brief's $85 an hour, and ties every claim to a message and a verbatim quote. One command, /observe, runs it; the outputs of one real run are committed beside the code, and this site is a reading copy of both.
